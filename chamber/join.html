Membership levels

NP membership

No fee

For non profit organizations serving the Valinhos community.

Bronze membership

R$ 90 / month

For small businesses taking their first steps with the chamber.

Silver membership

R$ 180 / month

For growing businesses that want more visibility.

Gold membership

R$ 320 / month

For established businesses that want to lead the local economy.

NP membership benefits

  • No membership fee for registered non profit organizations
  • Listing in the chamber directory
  • Access to all free chamber networking events
  • Monthly community newsletter

Bronze membership benefits

  • Everything in the NP level
  • 10% discount on chamber training workshops
  • Two event tickets per year
  • Business listing with logo in the directory

Silver membership benefits

  • Everything in the Bronze level
  • 25% discount on chamber training workshops
  • Spotlight position on the chamber home page
  • One free stand at the annual fig harvest festival
  • Invitation to the quarterly business leaders lunch

Gold membership benefits

  • Everything in the Silver level
  • 50% discount on chamber training workshops
  • Permanent spotlight position on the chamber home page
  • Logo on all chamber event materials
  • One sponsored article per year in the community newsletter
  • A seat on the chamber advisory board

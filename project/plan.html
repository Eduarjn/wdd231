Packing by difficulty

Easy

Water and sun protection. Trainers are enough on these surfaces.

Moderate

Boots with ankle support, two litres of water per person, and a jacket. The climb will take longer than the distance suggests.

Hard

Everything above plus food, a headlamp and an offline map. Tell someone where you are going and when you expect to be back.

One rule for all three

Turn around at your planned time, not at the summit. The accidents in this region happen on the way down, in the dark.

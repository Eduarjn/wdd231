About the trail information

This site was built as a student project for WDD 231 at BYU-Idaho. The places named are real locations in the Campinas region of Sao Paulo, Brazil, but the distances, walking times, elevation figures and access rules were compiled for this coursework and have not been verified against an official source.

Do not use this site to plan a real hike. Check the managing park or reserve for current access hours, trail conditions and rules before going.

Images
